# Research Decisions

## Available baseline

The first validated execution is `2026-Q3`, with an information cut-off of 1 September 2026. It includes a 15-company universe, FY2025 ranking, 2030 projection, CRM landscape, bilingual visual artifact, and confirmed notification.

## Durable methodology decisions

### Primary metric

The ranking uses net sales attributable to prescription medicines and vaccines. MedTech, Diagnostics, Consumer Health, Animal Health, Aesthetics, distribution, and services are excluded whenever they can be separated reliably.

### Ranking versus momentum

The ranking uses only the latest complete fiscal year. YTD/LTM results, guidance, and subsequent events remain in a separate monitor and influence only interpretation and projections.

### Foreign-exchange conversion

Companies are compared in USD using annual average exchange rates for the relevant fiscal period whenever possible. Close positions affected by FX are treated as uncertain.

### Forecasts

Company guidance, third-party forecasts, and own estimates are labelled separately. Future position is presented as a range when sales ranges overlap or the expected difference is below 5%.

### CRM

Classification requires a public source that identifies the company and platform. Global scope is declared only when the source makes it explicit. Vendor evidence is acceptable, but confidence is reduced when scope or rollout status is omitted.

### Quarterly materiality

A change is material when expected sales move by more than 5% or USD 2 billion, projected rank changes by at least two places, guidance or consensus changes significantly, a transaction could affect the ranking, a clinical or regulatory event changes commercial probability, a new loss of exclusivity emerges, or a new corporate CRM decision becomes public.

## 2026-Q3 baseline decisions

- AbbVie was adjusted to exclude Aesthetics; rankings that retain this business may place it higher.
- J&J was limited to Innovative Medicine, Roche to Pharmaceuticals, and Merck/MSD excluded Animal Health.
- AstraZeneca, Merck/MSD, and BMS retained pharmaceutical alliance or royalty revenue reported within the relevant business.
- The 2030 projection was recorded as an own range-based estimate anchored in sector sources and guidance, not as a precise consensus.
- Novo Nordisk ranked outside the FY2025 top 10 but was the leading entry candidate.
- Novo Nordisk’s Veeva Vault CRM decision was limited to International Operations and was not classified as global.

## Questions to revisit each quarter

- Corporate perimeter changes caused by acquisitions, divestments, or reporting changes.
- Availability of a new complete fiscal year to replace FY2025.
- Revised 2030 targets and directly accessible consensus sources.
- New evidence about CRM migrations and rollout completion.
- Continued public access to the stable URL and continuity of the Sites project.
